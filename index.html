Built around one good cup

Kettle & Cherry started in 2019 as a cart with one grinder. Today we run a roastery and a bar, and we still taste every batch before it ships.

    From bean to cup in four steps

    1

    Source

    We visit each farm and pick lots by cupping score.

    2

    Roast

    Each lot roasts for 9 to 12 minutes in a 12 kg drum.

    3

    Grind

    We grind to order, so aroma stays in the cup.

    4

    Pour

    Water at 93°C, a 28 second shot, milk steamed to 60°C.
